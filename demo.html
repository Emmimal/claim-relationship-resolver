Two claims can look different without disagreeing, or agree in wording while genuinely conflicting. Four systems answer each question below: my resolver (deterministic, no LLM), B_newest (newest claim wins), B+scope (newest claim wins within the matching scope), and A_retrieval (plain BM25 keyword search).
